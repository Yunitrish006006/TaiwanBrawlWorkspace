密文備援
A device ── WebRTC pairing ── B device
A device ── conversation key establishment ── B device
A device ── plaintext event ── P2P DataChannel ── B device
A device ── encrypt → ciphertext + nonce ── Worker / D1 pending relay
B device ── poll ciphertext ── decrypt → verify → durable ingest → ACK
